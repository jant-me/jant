A post's **…** menu has **Share as image**. It draws the post as a picture to save or send: the post in your site's fonts and colors on a mat tinted from your theme, with the site name, the post's address, and a QR code that opens it. The picture follows the summary the timeline shows, carries up to four images, and lists documents, recordings, and text attachments by name. A post in a Thread shows the Thread's first post above it and the next post below, faded, with its place in the Thread beside the date. The browser draws it, 1620 px wide. The menu offers it for published posts that aren't Private.
